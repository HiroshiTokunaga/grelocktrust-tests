What we cover in your first session

We go through the four places where most trouble starts. You make each change on your own phone and computer while your advisor explains what it does and why it matters.

  1. Your phone

    Face ID or fingerprint, automatic updates, and "Find My" so a lost or stolen phone can be locked or erased.

  2. Your web browser

    Warnings for fake websites, safer connections, and turning off the pop-up notifications scammers like to use.

  3. Your important accounts

    Email, bank, shopping and payment apps like Venmo or Zelle. We add a second check at login and set up alerts so you hear about any purchase or transfer right away.

  4. Your passwords

    We find any passwords that are weak, repeated, or have shown up in a data breach, and help you fix the ones that matter most.

You are never required to change anything. And you can ask about anything else that worries you along the way.

Common questions

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Most of our clients aren't. If you can join a Zoom call, we can take it from there. If Zoom itself is new to you, tell us when you book and we'll help you get connected first.

What do I need for the session?

The phone and computer you use every day, a charger, and about 90 minutes. It helps to know how to sign in to your email and bank accounts.

Will this mean I can never be scammed?

No one can promise that, and we won't. What we can do is close the most common gaps and help you recognize warning signs, so you're much better protected and know what to do if something looks wrong.

How do I know this email or link is really from you?

Good question to ask. We will never ask for your passwords, and we will never ask you to send money anywhere other than the $200 booking payment on this page. If something feels off, write to us directly before clicking.

How do refunds work?

Tell us you'd like a refund, by email or by replying to your confirmation, and we return the full $200 to the card you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
