Common questions

What's the difference between the two sessions?

The free, 20-minute Welcome Session is a conversation. You meet your advisor, learn why your online privacy and safety matter, and hear what the full session covers. Nothing on your devices is changed. In the 90-minute Digital Safety Session, you and your advisor check and strengthen your settings together.

What happens in the full session?

Over about 90 minutes on Zoom, your advisor walks you through the safety and privacy settings on your phone, computer, email and important accounts. You make each change yourself while your advisor explains what it does and why it matters. Afterward, you get a one-page summary of what you did and why.

Can you help with other tech questions?

Yes. We start with what matters most, your safety and privacy online. For anything else in your digital life, tell your advisor, and we'll book a separate session for it.

Can I book a session for my parent?

Yes. Use "Give a session to someone you love" on this page. You choose the time and pay, and we call them before the session to introduce ourselves. If they'd rather not go ahead, we'll refund you in full.

Who are the advisors?

Williams College students, chosen for patience and for explaining things clearly, and trained by the Williams alumni who lead Greylock Trust.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can join a Zoom call, we can take it from there. If Zoom itself is new to you, tell us when you book and we'll help you get connected first.

What do I need for the full session?

The phone and computer you use every day, a charger, and about 90 minutes. It helps to know how to sign in to your email and bank accounts.

Will this mean I can never be scammed?

No one can promise that, and we won't. We close the most common gaps and help you recognize warning signs, so you're much better protected and know what to do if something looks wrong.

How do I know an email or link is really from you?

We will never ask for your passwords, and we will never ask you to send money anywhere other than the booking payment on this page. If something feels off, write to us directly before clicking.

How do refunds work?

Tell us you'd like a refund, by email or by replying to your confirmation, and we return the full $200 to the card you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it. The same applies if you need to cancel before your session.
