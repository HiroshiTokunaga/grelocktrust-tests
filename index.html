Common questions

What happens in a session?

Over about 90 minutes on Zoom, your advisor goes through your phone, your web browser, your important accounts and your passwords with you. You make each change yourself while your advisor explains what it does and why it matters.

Who are the advisors?

Williams College students, chosen for patience and for explaining things clearly, and trained by the Williams alumni who lead Greylock Trust.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can join a Zoom call, we can take it from there. If Zoom itself is new to you, tell us when you book and we'll help you get connected first.

What do I need for the session?

The phone and computer you use every day, a charger, and about 90 minutes. It helps to know how to sign in to your email and bank accounts.

Will this mean I can never be scammed?

No one can promise that, and we won't. We close the most common gaps and help you recognize warning signs, so you're much better protected and know what to do if something looks wrong.

How do I know an email or link is really from you?

We will never ask for your passwords, and we will never ask you to send money anywhere other than the $200 booking payment on this page. If something feels off, write to us directly before clicking.

How do refunds work?

Tell us you'd like a refund, by email or by replying to your confirmation, and we return the full $200 to the card you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it. The same applies if you need to cancel before your session.
